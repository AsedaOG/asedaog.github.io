Featured case study

Credit Card Customer Churn: Prediction & Analysis

Analysed ~10,127 credit card customers to uncover why 16.1% had already churned, then trained a Gradient Boosting model that catches 93.9% of customers who actually leave (ROC-AUC 0.99). The strongest churn signals were falling transaction activity and revolving balance, followed by a spike in service contacts before exit. The model flags 292 currently active customers as High Risk today — worth roughly $715K in combined annual transaction volume — with a ranked list ready for retention outreach.
